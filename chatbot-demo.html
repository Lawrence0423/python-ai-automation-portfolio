What this shows

This simulates the customer-facing behavior of a support chatbot: it matches user questions to an approved FAQ knowledge base and falls back safely when confidence is low.

For a real client

  • The FastAPI version can connect to CRM, order, ticket, or helpdesk APIs.
  • Approved FAQ answers stay consistent.
  • Low-confidence questions can route to human review.

Why static here
